The reality

In Greece, there is no such thing as a “hotel licence.”

The right to open and operate a hotel is the cumulative alignment of title, zoning, buildability, environmental and cultural approvals, fire safety, hotel specifications, star classification, the OpenBusiness notification — and the funding conditions layered on top. One layer missed, or handled in the wrong order, can trap capital, shrink the room count, or stall an opening indefinitely.

Where deals break

Seven layers. Any one of them can break the investment.

01

Title & Cadastre

Ownership gaps, encumbrances, boundary disputes or access rights can delay — or defeat — the acquisition.

02

Zoning & buildability

Hotel use may be prohibited, capacity capped, or an off-plan plot may lack lawful road frontage.

03

Environmental & cultural protection

Natura, forestry, archaeology, coastline, streams and water infrastructure may reduce or prevent development.

04

Building & fire safety

Change of use, escape routes, accessibility and structural interventions can materially reduce the viable room count.

05

Hotel specifications & classification

The intended star category requires both mandatory criteria and sufficient scoring — design decisions determine classification.

06

Opening & operations

OpenBusiness is a notification regime, not a waiver of the underlying permits and continuing compliance duties.

07

Funding & state aid

Starting works or entering irreversible commitments too early may destroy eligibility for an incentive programme.

The method

One method. Seven stages. The whole investment lifecycle.

We do not treat licensing as a final administrative filing. We legally design and protect the project from the moment a site or building is considered until the hotel is open, operating — and ready to expand or exit.

01Investment structure & market entry+
  • Selection and establishment of the Greek investment vehicle or project SPV.
  • Shareholders' arrangements, governance, delegation of authority and board support.
  • Coordination of tax, VAT, accounting, banking and beneficial-ownership requirements.
  • Structuring of ownership, lease, management or franchise models and asset ring-fencing.
02Opportunity screening — before commitment+
  • Rapid red-flag screening of a plot, hotel or commercial building before exclusivity, deposit or extensive design work.
  • Preliminary review of zoning, use, buildability, capacity, access, environmental constraints and intended category.
  • Identification of deal-breakers, curable issues and matters requiring technical verification.
  • A concise go / conditional go / no-go recommendation for management.
03Acquisition & legal due diligence+
  • Title chain, Cadastre, encumbrances, easements, claims, leases and third-party rights.
  • Planning history, building permits, unauthorised works and Building Identity records.
  • Forestry, archaeology, Natura, coastline, former seashore, streams and protected-area review.
  • Heads of terms, exclusivity, preliminary agreements and sale-and-purchase agreements.
  • Conditions precedent, seller warranties, indemnities, price retentions, escrow and walk-away rights.
04Regulatory strategy, design & permitting+
  • A project-specific regulatory matrix: every approval, adviser, authority, dependency and deadline.
  • Legal review of design assumptions against intended use, capacity and star category.
  • Coordination with architects, engineers and planners on planning, environmental, fire-safety, accessibility and utility requirements.
  • Management of the Archaeological Service, Forestry Service, coastal, environmental, municipal and tourism authorities.
  • Legal support for permit amendments, capacity changes, mixed uses and phasing.
05Construction, procurement & delivery+
  • Architect, engineer, project-manager and consultant appointments.
  • Construction, fit-out, MEP, FF&E and specialist-supplier agreements.
  • Milestones, payment certification, performance security, delay damages, variations, defects and handover.
  • Interface management between design, licensing, funding and operator requirements.
  • Claims prevention — and negotiation, mediation, arbitration or litigation where required.
06Classification, opening & operational readiness+
  • Pre-classification audit against mandatory criteria and required points for the intended star category.
  • Coordination of the classification inspection and certificate process.
  • Opening-readiness file and the OpenBusiness operating notification.
  • Ancillary procedures: pools, food & beverage, spa, gym, music, events.
  • Opening checklist: guest terms, privacy, employment, health & safety, insurance, operational contracts.
07Ongoing operations, growth & exit+
  • Hotel management, franchise, lease and technical-services agreements.
  • OTA, distribution, direct-booking, cancellation and rate-parity terms.
  • Seasonal employment, working-time and HR compliance.
  • GDPR, CCTV, guest data, marketing consents and processor agreements.
  • Guest claims, inspections, administrative disputes and incident response.
  • Expansion, rebranding, refinancing, further acquisitions — or disposal.
Three ways in

Conversion, upgrade, or raw land — each route has its own trap.

Workstream A — Building conversion
60 rooms on the spreadsheet. 42 rooms under the law.

A commercial building can sit in a zone where hotel use is permitted — and still fail as a viable conversion once fire escapes, accessibility, circulation and minimum room sizes are applied. Our Hotel Conversion Feasibility Review answers the question before you buy.

What we do
  • Confirm the intended hotel or alternative accommodation use is legally permitted.
  • Review permit history, lawful use and any unauthorised works.
  • Test stars-based vs keys-based operating models.
  • Coordinate a technical gap analysis: room sizes, light, lifts, parking, escape routes, MEP capacity.
  • Verify the target key count survives compliance interventions.
  • Make the acquisition conditional on the results.
Workstream B — Star upgrade
A fourth star is not a renovation. It is a legal re-qualification.

Every mandatory criterion of the new category must be met, and the required points achieved — while the upgrade may reduce room count, raise staffing obligations and demand a new fire-safety solution. It must be evaluated as one legal, technical and financial model.

What we do
  • Review the operating file, classification certificate and actual configuration.
  • Prepare a criterion-by-criterion classification gap matrix.
  • Identify mandatory works, service changes and scoring options.
  • Determine which works need permits or revised approvals.
  • Coordinate the new inspection and certificate.
  • Check eligibility for integrated-modernisation incentives.
Workstream C — Land acquisition
There is no such thing as a “hotel-licensed plot.”

A visually perfect plot may be unbuildable: the road may not be legally public, protected zones may consume the usable area, or water and wastewater infrastructure may never support the intended capacity. We assess the probability that your hotel can lawfully exist there.

What we do
  • Verify title, boundaries, Cadastre status, easements and lawful access.
  • Confirm permitted tourism use, buildability and bed-capacity limits.
  • Review forest maps, Natura, archaeology, coastline, streams and flood risk.
  • Assess utilities and access-road feasibility.
  • Coordinate a preliminary master plan to test real capacity.
  • Draft transaction documents with project-specific conditions precedent.
Funding & incentives

Money is available. Sequencing decides who receives it.

Development Law 4887/2022

New or expanded hotels of at least four stars, integrated modernisation of three-star-plus hotels, certain closed hotels, and projects in traditional or listed buildings — subject to the applicable call.

Recovery & Resilience Facility

Loan financing for qualifying green, digital and expansion components, subject to bank and programme requirements.

NSRF & regional programmes

NSRF calls, LEADER/CLLD and other time-limited programmes, where location, activity code, size and project type are eligible.

Strategic investments

Fast-track procedures for sufficiently large or qualifying projects.

State-aid compliance

Regional-aid limits, de minimis, cumulation and “incentive effect” sequencing — coordinated with tax structuring, VAT, ENFIA and SPV design.

“Starting works — or signing one irreversible commitment — a week too early can void eligibility for an entire incentive programme.”

We sequence every acquisition, contract and works commencement so that never happens. And because a statutory regime is not the same as an open application window, we confirm eligibility, aid intensity and deadlines for each project before anything binding is signed.
